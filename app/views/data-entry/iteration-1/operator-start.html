Report Pollution Release and Transfer Register (PRTR) data

Use this service to report annual data for the UK Pollutant Release and Transfer Register (PRTR).

You can report data for:

  • pollutant releases to air, water or land
  • pollutant transfers to wastewater
  • waste transfers

Who needs to report

The UK PRTR legislation sets out industrial activities are covered.

Operators of industrial facilities that carry out these activities must report relevant pollutant releases and waste transfers each year.

Annex 2 of the legislation gives a list of pollutants that must be reported on for PRTR.

After you submit your data

Your report will be reviewed by the relevant regulatory authority – for example, your local authority or national regulator.

They will contact you to let you know if your report has been approved or if they need you to provide more information.
